# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Vitalis (the "App") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is operated by [LLC LEGAL NAME], a [STATE] limited liability company ("we", "us"). Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Summary

- We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
- The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Most of your logs (meals, water, raw-food batches, biomarkers, sun sessions) stay on your device.
- Data read from Apple Health is never used for advertising and is never sold.
- You can delete your account and server data at any time from Settings.

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account:** your email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider), or the identifier and name provided by Sign in with Apple or Google.
- **Profile:** age group, sex, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, weight, height, activity level, the location you type in (such as a city), and diet type. We use this to calculate your calorie and nutrient targets.
- **Social features:** username, display name, bio, profile photo, friend requests and friendships, and the meals you choose to share.
- **Support messages:** anything you send us by email.

### Information stored only on your device

Meal logs, water and salt intake, raw-food batches and suppliers, biomarker readings, check-ins and sun sessions are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, except meals you share as described below.

### Information from your device

- **Camera and photos:** when you scan food, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to estimate what the food is and its nutrition. We do not store the photo. Barcodes are looked up with Open Food Facts.
- **Location:** with your permission, your precise location is used on your device to show nearby butchers and farms (map searches are handled by Apple Maps) and to calculate sun position (your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo). We do not store your precise location on our servers.
- **Apple Health:** with your permission, the App reads steps, active energy, time in daylight, body mass, body fat, waist circumference, blood pressure, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and blood glucose, and writes dietary energy, protein and water. Health data is used only to provide features in the App. It is not uploaded to our servers, not used for advertising or marketing, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

### Payment information

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never receive your card number or billing address. The App receives the subscription's product, purchase and expiry dates, and a transaction identifier from Apple to check that your subscription is active. If you are signed in when you subscribe, your account's user ID is attached to the purchase so the subscription can be linked to your account.

## Meal sharing

When you set up your social profile, your meals are visible to your friends by default. You can change this to "Nobody" or "Everyone" in Settings at any time. Shared meals include the food name, calories, macronutrients, serving size and time eaten. Turning sharing off stops future meals from being shared.

## How we use information

- To provide the App: your account, nutrient targets, food recognition, search, maps, sun tracking and social features.
- To process subscriptions and provide customer support.
- To keep the App secure and prevent abuse.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

## Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the App, and only as needed for the purpose described:

- **Supabase** – account authentication, database and file storage.
- **Google (Gemini API)** – food photo analysis. Google Sign-In, if you choose it.
- **Apple** – App Store subscriptions and payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps and Apple Health.
- **Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, Open-Meteo, USDA FSIS and FDA** – public food, weather and recall data. We send search text, barcodes or coordinates, not your account information.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or as part of a merger, sale or reorganization, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account and profile data while your account is active. You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, social data, shared meals and profile photo from our servers. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the App. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its privacy policy.
